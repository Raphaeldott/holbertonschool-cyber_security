# 11x00. Four Letters, One Client

Governance, Risk & Compliance project. Working as a Junior GRC Consultant at
Northbridge Risk Advisory, mapping the client **Nordwell Technologies** to its
obligations across four frameworks: **GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS, and SOC 2**.

The deliverable chain starts from facts (intake) and ends with a one-page answer
for the CEO: which obligations are legally mandatory, which are contractual, which
can wait, and what to do first.

> Scope: only GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS and SOC 2. ISO 27001 and NIST CSF are out of scope.
> Every penalty figure, version number, or legal claim must be verifiable in the
> project Resources — no folklore.

## Deliverables

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `0-client_profile.md` | Intake sheet: compliance-relevant facts, before naming any framework |
| `3-framework_matrix.md` | 4×6 comparison matrix + "Status as of 2026" |
| `5-client_memo.md` | One-page memo to the CEO: mandatory / contractual / can wait, and Monday actions |

All deliverables are in English, in Markdown, and end with a newline.
